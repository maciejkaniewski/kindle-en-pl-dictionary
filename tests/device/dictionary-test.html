Dictionary test

A test book for the English–Polish Kindle dictionary (kindle-en-pl-dictionary).

Bold word: long-press it. Underlined words: drag-select all of them. The expected result is shown after the arrow.

1. Past tense and plurals

She gave me her old bike. → give: meanings first; “Czasowniki frazowe” (give away … give up) at the end of the entry

We went home early. → go

The children were asleep. → child

Two mice ran under the table. → mouse (rzeczownik first: mysz)

He took the last train. → take

I bought some bread. → buy

This one is better. → good

2. Words that are also other words

The sun rose at six. → rose: “→ rise” first, then róża

He fell asleep. → fell: “→ fall” first

She woke before dawn. → woke: “→ wake” first
